# Audio credits

Phonetics Lab 2 plays 23 sound files. 12 of them come
from elsewhere and are listed below. Each is redistributed exactly as it was
received, with nothing trimmed, padded or re-encoded.

Everything else was made for this course: the remaining recordings were generated for
it, every illustration is drawn in the page as SVG, and the effect sounds are
synthesized in the browser.
