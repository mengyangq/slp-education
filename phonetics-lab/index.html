Phonetics Lab

Interactive practice for an undergraduate phonetics course. Hear, see and build the sounds of English, from morphemes and minimal pairs to vowels, consonants and IPA transcription. Every question is followed by the answer and a short explanation.

The labs

  • Lab 1

    Phonetics Playground

    Morphemes, minimal pairs, and counting the sounds in a word.

    Open lab 1 →
  • Lab 2

    The Speech Machine

    The three systems of speech, the vocal tract, and words built from their sounds.

    Open lab 2 →
  • Lab 3

    Where Sounds Are Made

    The articulators behind each consonant, and sounds read from a moving mouth.

    Open lab 3 →
  • Lab 4

    How Sounds Are Made

    Place, airflow and voicing, and words that differ by a single sound.

    Open lab 4 →
  • Lab 5

    The Vowel Map

    Vowels by ear, where the tongue goes for each one, and diphthongs.

    Open lab 5 →
  • Lab 6

    Sound to Symbol

    Consonants read from a moving mouth, and words written in IPA.

    Open lab 6 →
  • Lab 7

    Flow and Friction

    Affricates, liquids and glides, read from a moving mouth and written in IPA.

    Open lab 7 →
