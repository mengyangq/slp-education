    Processes and the age they usually go

    Each bar runs until the age by which the process is usually gone.

    Use these ages as guidelines, not cutoffs.
    • A process still there past its age is a reason to look closer. On its own it does not show a speech sound disorder.
    • Weigh the child's intelligibility, dialect, whole sound system and how consistent the errors are.
    • Children who speak more than one language or dialect may use patterns from them. Dialect differences are not errors.

    Consonants

    The process ages and these consonant ages come from different studies with different criteria. A child can stop using a pattern, such as stopping /s/, before the sound itself is fully accurate.
