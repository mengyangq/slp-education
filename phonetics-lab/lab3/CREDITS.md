## Figure

The drawn mouth's resting tongue is adapted from the cardinal vowel diagrams by Badseed on Wikimedia Commons ([Cardinal vowel tongue position-front.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cardinal_vowel_tongue_position-front.svg) and its back-vowel companion), licensed [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). The figure is shared under the same licence; the consonant shapes were drawn for this course.

from elsewhere and are listed below. Each is redistributed exactly as it was
received, with nothing trimmed, padded or re-encoded.

Everything else was made for this course: the remaining recordings were generated for
it, the rest of the drawing is SVG authored in the page (the word pictures are
images made for it), and the effect sounds are
synthesized in the browser.
