Reading the Scores

Eight fictional clients, each with results from the Comprehensive Aphasia Test (CAT). Choose any client, read the results and the errors, and build the summary a clinician would write in the chart.

  1. 1

    Read the results

    Each subtest is marked below the cut-off or within normal limits. Below the cut-off means lower than 95 percent of people without aphasia.

  2. 2

    Answer four questions

    Two on what the results show, then a chart paragraph to complete and its closing sentence. Retry as often as you like: a right first try earns a gem, a later try half a gem.

  3. 3

    Compare with the model

    Each client closes with the full chart summary.
